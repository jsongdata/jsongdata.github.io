## Motivation
CompTIA Security+ is often considered a foundational certification for entry-level cybersecurity roles.

## What does it include? (SY0-701)
1. General Security Concepts (12%)
2. Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations (22%)
3. Security Architecture (18%)
4. Security Operations (28%)
5. Security Program Management & Oversight (20%)

## Exam Cost, Date, and Method
The standard exam voucher costs $439 USD today. You can obtain discounts through the CompTIA Academic Store if you have a student email. To avoid paying renewal fees, you can simply earn a higher-level certification (such as CySA+ or CASP+) which automatically renews your Security+ status.

## How I am studying and how I feel about it

### Week 1-3
I tried to solve 25 problems or 1 hour a day otherwise it can quickly lead to burnout

### After week 3
After solving all problems at least once, it becomes easier to review on the second pass

### After week 5
I have covered approximately 50% of the exam objectives.

### On week 6
I started looking for a discounted voucher to schedule my exam date and location. Since the exam is quite expensive, I’m also considering whether I should go with the Retake Assurance option ($579).
