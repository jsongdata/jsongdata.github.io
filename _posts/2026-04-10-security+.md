## Definition
CompTIA Security+ is a foundational certification for entry-level cybersecurity roles.

## Exam Cost
The standard exam voucher costs $439 USD today.

## Get a Student Discount Here
https://help.comptia.org/hc/en-us/articles/38887439363092-What-Academic-Discounts-and-Purchasing-Options-Are-Available-for-Students-and-Institutions

## Exam Format
70-90 questions total. My exam had 76 questions

Multiple choice questions (90%) + performance-based questions (10%)


## Study Resources I used
* Professor Messer (Free Video Courses and Study Groups) 

## My progress and how I felt each week 

**Week 1-2**

I partially watched videos on topics I wasn’t familiar with and read Professor Messer’s textbook. I tried to study 1 hour a day consistently. Otherwise, it could quickly lead to burnout

**Week 3-4**

After solving all the problems at least once, reviewing them on the second pass became easier

**Week 5**

I felt consistently confident across more than 77% of the exam objectives

**Week 6**

I bought a discounted voucher and was trying to find the best day to take the exam. Since my graduate Computer Networks class starts next Monday, I needed to finish the exam before the weekend ended.

**On Saturday, May 16, 2026**
### I took the exam and I officially passed the exam!
On Saturday, I was quite flustered at the beginning of the exam because there were questions I didn’t expect to see. After passing the 60-question mark, my eyes became fatigued quickly because the font size was so small, and my vision actually started to blur.

Tip: The night before the exam, try not to study too hard. Instead, get plenty of rest.

When GPT-3 first hit the market in 2023, using generative AI for studying was still met with skepticism. For this exam, I made the most of Claude CLI and Codex to transform Professor Messer's study materials into organized notes, practice sets, and flashcard decks, allowing me to study more effectively and cover more ground. Going forward, I plan to lean into AI tools even more aggressively.
